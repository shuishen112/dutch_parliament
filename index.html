How these passages were found

Each passage carries the strongest rule it meets. Untick a rule to hide its passages. The rules were written from the hand-picked examples; they miss passages that say it in other words.

Spellings included in this search

Old print is often misread by the computer (for example s1aven for slaven), so the search also looks for spellings that differ by a letter or two. Spellings that are much more common than the word you typed are probably other words (anderen for kinderen), so they start unticked. Tick or untick as needed, then press Search again.

How to use this page
  1. Type one or more words and press Search. Several words must appear close together and in that order (kinderen uit slavinnen finds "de kinderen uit slavinnen geboren").
  2. To allow any order, put alternatives in curly brackets and add ~ and a number of words: {kinderen|kind} {slavinnen|slaven} {vrij|vrijverklaring} ~20 finds a word from each bracket within 20 words of each other (at most 60). A * stands for any letters: vrijverklar*, *kinderen.
  3. Free womb lists every passage found by the project's rules for the idea that children born to enslaved mothers should be free. "Strict" passages are the most likely; "loose" and "context" ones are mostly other topics.
  4. Each result shows the date, the document, the part of the document (long debates are cut into passages of about 300 words), and the text read by the computer. The text contains errors and sometimes mixes up the two columns of the page: always check the scan. Open the document leads to the scan (PDF) at officielebekendmakingen.nl. If the text is wrong, click Correct OCR, type what the print actually says, and save.
  5. For each result, answer the question with Yes, directly (it proposes, debates or rejects freeing those children), Related (how it would work in practice, e.g. guardianship of the children), No, or Unsure; add a note if you like.
  6. Download this list saves a spreadsheet of every hit with its OCR text and your labels. Download my checks saves only what you labelled or corrected.
  7. Put = before a word to turn off variant spellings for that word only (=vrij).

Only documents that mention slavery (a word containing slav, slaaf or lijfeigen) are included. Exact duplicate files and the subject registers are left out.
